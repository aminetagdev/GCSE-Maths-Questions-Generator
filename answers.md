# Answer Key - Pythagoras' Theorem

**1.** A right-angled triangle has shorter sides of 10 cm and 24 cm. Find the hypotenuse.
Answer: sqrt(10^2 + 24^2) = sqrt(676) = 26 cm

**2.** A right-angled triangle has a hypotenuse of 25 cm and one side of 24 cm. Find the missing side.
Answer: sqrt(25^2 - 24^2) = sqrt(49) = 7 cm

**3.** A right-angled triangle has shorter sides of 5 cm and 12 cm. Find the hypotenuse.
Answer: sqrt(5^2 + 12^2) = sqrt(169) = 13 cm
